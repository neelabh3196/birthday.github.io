For My Bubu ❤️

Maybe this whole website started as a little birthday surprise, but there is one thing that has never been a surprise to me — how much I love you.

Bubu, you mean so much more to me than I could ever properly put into words. There are so many things I love about you, so many little moments that I keep replaying in my head, and so many memories that I know I will carry with me for a very, very long time.

Sometimes I think about how all these little moments started becoming our memories. The random conversations, the stupid jokes, the laughter, the silly arguments, the food, the outings, the pictures, and all those tiny moments that probably seemed ordinary at the time.

But when I look back at them now, I realise that those ordinary moments were never really ordinary.

They were special because they were moments I got to spend with you. ❤️

You have become such a beautiful part of my life, and I genuinely feel lucky that I get to make memories with you. You make me laugh when I don't feel like laughing, you make normal days feel exciting, and somehow even doing nothing with you can become one of my favourite things.

I don't know what the future is going to look like, and I don't know how many birthdays, dates, trips, meals, photos, fights, laughs, and random conversations are waiting for us.

But I do know one thing.

I want more of them with you. ❤️

I want more random pictures. More food dates. More stupid jokes. More late conversations. More adventures. More moments where we look at each other and laugh for absolutely no reason.

I want more memories that we can look back on years from now and say, "Remember this?"

And most importantly, I want you to know that you are loved.

Not just today because it's your birthday. Not just because I made this website. Not just because I am writing this letter.

You are loved in all those tiny moments that you probably don't even notice.

In the way I remember little things about you. In the way I smile when I see a message from you. In the way certain places, songs, foods, jokes and random things remind me of you.

I hope this little surprise made you smile. I hope you enjoyed choosing everything. And I hope that when you look at these 22 memories, you remember not just the pictures, but the feelings behind them.

Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for every memory. Thank you for every laugh. Thank you for every little moment.

And thank you for being my Bubu. ❤️

Here's to you. Here's to us. And here's to a million more memories. 💖

Happy Birthday, Bubu. 🎂❤️

I love you more than you know.

Forever yours,
Me ❤️
